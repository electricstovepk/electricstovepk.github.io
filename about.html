About Electric Stove PK

Electric Stove PK publishes practical buying guides on electric stoves, hot plates and infrared cookers for Pakistani kitchens — prices, wattage, electricity cost and which type suits which home.

How we research

Glam Gas prices and specifications are taken from glamgas.com and dated on each page. Prices for other brands (Anex, Westpoint, Xiaomi and others) are the ranges we saw at Pakistani online retailers on the date shown. Electricity costs are worked out from the stove's wattage and typical Pakistani per-unit tariffs. We recheck prices regularly, and we say so when another brand is the cheaper choice.

Disclosure

This site is published in partnership with Glam Gas, a kitchen-appliance manufacturer in Korangi Industrial Area, Karachi, and features Glam Gas products. Specifications and prices are taken from glamgas.com and dated on each page. We do not publish invented reviews or ratings.
